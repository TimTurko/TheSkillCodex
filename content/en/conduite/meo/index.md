# MEO — Methods, organisation, facilitation

Handled jointly with [[gestion-de-projet-en|project planning and tracking]], which carries the steering of the team through the phases. The **MEO** domain gathers the collective working practices and the engineering disciplines that run through the whole project: traceability, communication, documentary rigour.

## Concepts covered

- [[archivage-projet-en|Project archiving]] — organising and keeping deliverables, code and documentation so that they stay findable
- [[relation-client-en|Client relationship]] — conducting the exchange with the sponsor and getting approval at the milestones
- [[revue-de-code-en|Code review]] — having the code read at the critical points, as a shared quality routine
- [[unite-si-en|SI units]] — the rigour of a number given with its unit in every deliverable
- [[cable-management-en|Cable management]] — organising and securing the wiring for reliability and diagnosis
